Our story

Software built by one studio, for one market it actually understands

LamaStudio started as a way to build the apps a Pakistani Android user actually needs — ones that work on an unreliable connection, respect a limited data plan, and let people pay the way they already pay for everything else.

Mission

Build genuinely useful Android software for the Pakistani market — apps that are fast on modest hardware, honest about the data they collect, and priced and billed in a way that doesn't assume a foreign credit card.

Vision

A small, sustainable catalog of tools people keep on their phone for years, not a pipeline of disposable apps chasing install numbers. Fewer apps, maintained properly, beats a large catalog nobody updates.

Background

LamaStudio is run by Wajid, who splits his time between software development and academic research in education, including M.Phil-level research work. That research background shows up directly in the studio's education-focused tools — result-card generators and school management software built around how Pakistani schools actually operate, from student ID conventions to fee-voucher formats.

On the engineering side, the work spans native Android (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose), Laravel-based web and SaaS platforms, and browser-based tools — all built and maintained directly, without an agency layer in between.

Values

  • Local first — Pakistani payment rails, Pakistani market conventions, and Urdu-English patterns aren't an afterthought.
  • Minimal permissions — apps request only what they need, and processing stays on-device wherever that's realistic.
  • Direct accountability — support requests and bug reports go straight to the person who wrote the code.
