Who we are

About LamaStudio

LamaStudio is a small, independent Android studio. There's no outsourcing chain and no product committee — every app in this store was designed and built by the same team that answers your support email.

Mission

Build software that fits how people in Pakistan actually pay, read, and plan their day — local billing rails, real bilingual support, and calendars that don't assume a single dating system — without compromising on the engineering quality expected from any global app store listing.

Vision

A studio that can stand next to any international developer on craft, while staying rooted in the region it was built in — proving that "built in Pakistan" is a mark of quality, not a compromise.

What we've shipped

Privacy & networking

LamaVPN Pro, built on the WireGuard protocol with security-forward architecture and Android Keystore-backed key storage.

Productivity

LamaSky's multi-calendar engine spanning Gregorian, Hijri, and Nanakshahi systems, with home-screen widgets that stay in sync.

Education

LamaIQMaster, a bilingual, adaptive-difficulty practice app for grades 1 through 5.

Research grounding

Our education-focused work is informed by ongoing M.Phil. research into Pakistani education policy, so LamaIQMaster's design choices are grounded in more than intuition.
