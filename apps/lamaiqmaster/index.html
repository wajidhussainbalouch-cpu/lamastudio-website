LamaIQMaster

Practice that gets harder only when your child is ready.

A grades 1–5 question bank in Urdu and English, with an adaptive difficulty engine that responds to how a child is actually doing — not a fixed lesson plan.

  • ✓Bilingual question bank for grades 1–5
  • ✓Adaptive difficulty engine tuned per learner
  • ✓Cross-platform via Capacitor — Android, iOS, and PC
  • ✓Subscription gating built on RevenueCat

Under the hood

Adaptive engine

Difficulty adjusts question-by-question based on recent accuracy and response time, aiming to keep a child in a productive challenge zone instead of frustration or boredom.

Bilingual by design

Question content, instructions, and feedback are authored in both Urdu and English from the start, not machine-translated after the fact.

Cross-platform reach

Built with Capacitor, React, and TypeScript so the same core app runs on Android, iOS, and desktop from one codebase.

Research-informed content

Question design draws on ongoing M.Phil. research into Pakistani education policy, so the grade-level pacing reflects real classroom expectations.
