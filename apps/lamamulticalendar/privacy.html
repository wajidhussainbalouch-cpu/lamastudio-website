Privacy Policy for LamaMultiCalendar

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

At LamaStudio, we take your personal data and scheduling security seriously. LamaMultiCalendar is built with an offline-first philosophy, designed to handle your calendar events and multi-system date calculations (Gregorian, Hijri, and Nanakshahi) securely on your local device.

2. Local Data Storage & Event Privacy

Your calendar events, notes, reminders, and custom configurations are stored locally on your device. We do not harvest, upload, or monetize your personal schedule entries or entries stored within local calendar databases.

3. Multi-Calendar Conversion Engine

All calendar synchronization and date transformations between Gregorian, Hijri, and Nanakshahi systems are handled algorithmically within the app runtime environment without requiring your personal details to be broadcasted to external trackers.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

To support ongoing development and app maintenance, LamaMultiCalendar may incorporate standard advertising or diagnostic frameworks (such as Google AdMob) which operate under their respective independent privacy frameworks.

5. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be revised occasionally to match new feature rollouts or platform updates. Continued use of the application indicates acceptance of the terms outlined here.
