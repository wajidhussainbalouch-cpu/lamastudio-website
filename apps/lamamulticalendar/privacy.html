Privacy Policy for LamaMultiCalendar

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview & Our Commitment

At LamaStudio, we take your personal schedule and calendar security seriously. LamaMultiCalendar is engineered with an offline-first priority to ensure that your everyday events, multi-system dates, and time notes remain completely under your own control.

2. Local Data Storage & Event Privacy

LamaMultiCalendar stores your daily appointments, reminders, and customizations entirely on your local device storage. We explicitly do not upload, harvest, or monetize:

  • Your personal calendar events, memos, or task descriptions.
  • Specific local database entries or unique scheduling histories.
  • Personal user markers or individual lifestyle time patterns.

3. Multi-System Date Calculation Engine

All algorithm-driven transformations between Gregorian, Hijri, and Nanakshahi (Sikh Punjabi) calendar formats are executed directly inside the local runtime environment of your device.

Because conversion happens locally, your active schedule configuration is never transmitted across external networks or saved to centralized servers.

4. Minimal Diagnostics & App Performance

To ensure smooth performance across various screen sizes and handle structural updates seamlessly, we process only basic, non-identifiable usage logs:

  • General crash reports or system exceptions handled via standard platform tools.
  • Basic device language and layout orientation settings for interface optimization.

This technical metadata is completely anonymous and never mapped to your identity or calendar entries.

5. Third-Party Services & Monetization

LamaMultiCalendar may integrate standard advertising or diagnostic frameworks (such as Google AdMob) to sustain development updates, operating strictly under their respective standalone privacy guidelines.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to match new feature rollouts or platform enhancements. Modifications take effect immediately upon being posted to this section.
