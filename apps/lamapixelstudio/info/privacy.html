Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Local Processing Guarantee

At lamaPixelStudio, your privacy is our core architecture. All image resizing, compression, cropping, and format conversions take place entirely on your own local device using client-side Web APIs and HTML5 Canvas. **Your images are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external servers.**

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, track, or share personal information or user files. Any operational preferences you set (such as theme toggles) are stored locally in your browser's session or local storage.

3. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please feel free to reach out via our Contact page.
