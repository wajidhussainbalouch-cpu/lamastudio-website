Privacy Policy for LamaSky

Effective Date: July 25, 2026 | Developer: LamaStudio

At LamaStudio, we build dynamic, utility-focused applications like LamaSky with user trust and data security as our core foundation. This Privacy Policy outlines how LamaSky handles your information when you download, install, and interact with our application and live widgets.

Core Summary: LamaSky is engineered with a local-first architecture. We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) on external cloud servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your personal space and digital footprint. LamaSky does not require you to register an account, sign in, or submit personal credentials. Consequently, we do not gather:

  • Your full name, home address, or telephone number.
  • Your personal email address or communication logs.
  • Private device contacts, photos, or media files.

2. Data Handled Locally on Your Device

To provide real-time temperature tracking, time updates, and weather widgets on your device screen, LamaSky processes certain technical metrics locally:

  • Location Metrics: If you grant location permission, it is used solely on your device to fetch live meteorological updates from trusted open-source weather networks. Your coordinate data is never broadcast to LamaStudio servers.
  • Widget Preferences: Your customized layout choices, temperature units (Celsius/Fahrenheit), and refresh intervals are saved strictly within local device storage.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

LamaSky may utilize industry-standard third-party frameworks (such as Google Play Services or AdMob/AdSense integrations if ads are enabled) to ensure app stability and crash-free execution. These third parties may automatically collect non-personal operational data, such as:

  • Anonymous diagnostic logs and crash reports.
  • General device architecture type and operating system version.
  • Broad geographic region for localized ad delivery or server load balancing.

These third-party providers operate under their own independent privacy policies.

4. Children’s Privacy

LamaSky is designed as a family-friendly weather utility. We do not knowingly target, solicit, or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided unauthorized data to an integrated network, please contact us immediately for prompt removal.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in LamaSky or adjustments in regulatory compliance. Any modifications will be updated directly on this page with a revised effective date.
