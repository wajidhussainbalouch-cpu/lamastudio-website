Privacy Policy for LamaSky

Effective Date: July 25, 2026 • Engineered By: LamaStudio

Core Architecture Guarantee: LamaSky is built with absolute privacy-first, local storage paradigms. We do not aggregate or harvest personal metrics on cloud servers.

1. Zero Personal Data Harvesting

At LamaStudio, we believe your digital footprint belongs exclusively to you. LamaSky does not mandate account creation, user authentication portals, or credential submissions. Consequently, our ecosystem completely avoids collecting:

  • Legal names, home addresses, or cellular contact details.
  • Private email correspondence or messaging logs.
  • Sensitive local device storage, photos, or media databases.

2. Local-First Technical Processing

To render real-time meteorological metrics, precise clock adjustments, and live widget panels seamlessly on your device screen, LamaSky handles technical criteria strictly on-device:

  • Coordinate Services: If location permissions are granted, coordinates are consumed locally to pull weather streams from verified open APIs. They are never mirrored to LamaStudio mainframes.
  • Interface Preferences: Configuration choices, unit scaling (Celsius/Fahrenheit), and layout adjustments persist securely within isolated local application storage.

3. Third-Party Telemetry & Analytics

To safeguard software performance, eliminate application crashes, and ensure system compatibility across complex device hardware, LamaSky may integrate standard telemetry frameworks (such as Google Play Services or AdMob networks). These systems may capture anonymous runtime metrics:

  • Aggregated diagnostic diagnostics and crash logs.
  • General hardware specification and operating system version IDs.
  • Broad regional identifiers optimized for network content delivery.

4. Minor & Family Security

LamaSky operates as an accessible family weather utility. We do not intentionally track, solicit, or gather information from minors under 13. If unauthorized telemetry submission is identified, contact our operations desk immediately for rapid data scrubbing.

5. Policy Revisions

This privacy declaration may evolve dynamically to mirror feature upgrades or policy expansions. All future modifications will be published directly to this URL alongside a refreshed effective timestamp.
