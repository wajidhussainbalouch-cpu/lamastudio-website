LamaStudio Weather App Privacy Policy

LamaSky Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At LamaStudio, we respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy specifically governs the LamaSky weather forecasting application and outlines how we handle your data when you use our weather tracking mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect for Weather Services

LamaSky is designed with weather tracking and privacy at its core. We minimize data collection to ensure your weather forecasting experience is secure, lightweight, and efficient:

  • Location Coordinates: To provide accurate, hyper-local weather forecasting, live radar tracking, and atmospheric updates, LamaSky requests access to your device's location data. This location information is processed exclusively to fetch real-time meteorological metrics for your specific region and is never stored or tracked for advertising or marketing profiles.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Logs: We collect non-identifying technical logs and crash reports related strictly to weather data fetching stability, allowing us to maintain app reliability and optimize low battery overhead synchronization algorithms.

2. How We Use Your Weather Data

The limited data processed by LamaSky is utilized strictly for the following operational weather services:

  • To fetch and render real-time temperature, precipitation metrics, and live radar data tailored directly to your current or searched location.
  • To optimize background weather polling intervals, ensuring your atmospheric updates remain current without draining your device's battery resources.
  • To troubleshoot weather data synchronization bugs and enhance overall application reliability.

3. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize your location data or weather tracking habits to any third parties. All network requests and communication sessions between the LamaSky app and meteorological servers are fully encrypted.

4. Changes to This Weather App Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy for LamaSky from time to time. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or how LamaSky handles weather data, please reach out to us at LamaStudio, DG Khan, Pakistan via lamastudio.pk.
