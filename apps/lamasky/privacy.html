LamaSky Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At LamaStudio, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how LamaSky handles your data when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

LamaSky is designed with privacy at its core. We minimize data collection to ensure your weather tracking experience is secure and lightweight:

  • Location Data: To provide hyper-local weather forecasting and atmospheric updates, LamaSky may request access to your device's coarse or precise location coordinates. This information is processed exclusively to fetch regional weather intelligence and is never stored or tracked for advertising profiles.
  • Usage & Performance Logs: We may collect non-identifying diagnostic logs and crash reports to maintain application stability and improve our low battery overhead optimization algorithms.

2. How We Use Your Information

The limited data we process is used solely for the following operational purposes:

  • To fetch and render real-time live radar and temperature metrics tailored to your selected or current location.
  • To optimize background synchronization polling intervals for maximum battery efficiency.
  • To troubleshoot software bugs and enhance overall app reliability.

3. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or location metrics to third parties. All communication requests between LamaSky and weather servers are encrypted securely.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or LamaSky's data practices, please reach out to us at LamaStudio, Islamabad, Pakistan via lamastudio.pk.
