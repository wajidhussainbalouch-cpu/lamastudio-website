Privacy Policy for LamaSky

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview & Our Commitment

At LamaStudio, we take your cloud data security and document privacy seriously. LamaSky is engineered with robust encryption standards and secure cloud infrastructure to ensure that your files, notes, and collaborative work remain completely protected and under your control.

2. Cloud Data Storage & Document Privacy

LamaSky stores your uploaded files, account credentials, and workspace customizations entirely within secure, encrypted cloud partitions. We explicitly do not sell, harvest, or monetize:

  • Your personal documents, notes, or media uploads.
  • Specific private workspace entries or individual cloud folder structures.
  • Personal user behavioral markers or individual search histories.

3. Secure Cloud Synchronization Engine

All algorithm-driven document transfers, real-time sync requests, and cloud backups are executed over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS) connections directly into secure storage nodes.

Because robust security protocols govern our sync architecture, your active workspace data is never exposed across unsecure networks or harvested by third-party intermediaries.

4. Minimal Diagnostics & App Performance

To ensure smooth performance across various screen sizes and handle structural updates seamlessly, we process only basic, non-identifiable usage logs:

  • General crash reports or system exceptions handled via standard platform tools.
  • Basic device language and layout orientation settings for interface optimization.

This technical metadata is completely anonymous and never mapped to your identity or private file contents.

5. Third-Party Services & Monetization

LamaSky may integrate standard advertising or diagnostic frameworks (such as Google AdMob) to sustain development updates, operating strictly under their respective standalone privacy guidelines.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to match new feature rollouts or platform enhancements. Modifications take effect immediately upon being posted to this section.
