LamaSky Privacy Notice

Last reviewed: 27 November 2026. This notice describes the public LamaSky weather-showcase page on lamastudio.pk. Features and data practices of a separately installed Android app may differ; consult its current store information and contact details for app-specific questions.

City search and sample readings

The website city-search field updates the browser display and map locations. In the reviewed website code, displayed temperature, humidity, wind and air-quality figures are predefined or generated sample values, not verified real-time readings. Please use an authoritative weather source for weather-related decisions.

Browser preferences

The public LamaSky page uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark display theme under the key lamastudio_theme. You can clear this preference using your browser's site-data controls. The reviewed website page does not request browser geolocation permission or submit its city-search field through an application API.

External map and radar services

The page embeds maps from OpenStreetMap and weather-map views from Windy. When these embedded services load, your browser connects to their servers, which may receive IP address, device/browser information and requests needed to display the map. Their own privacy notices and policies govern that processing.

Advertising, cookies and analytics

This privacy notice itself does not load an advertising script or an ad placement. The wider LAMA Studio website may use advertising or other third-party services on eligible public pages if enabled; details and visitor choices are described in the general privacy policy.

Questions and updates

For questions about this site and your information, use the contact channels described on the About LAMA Studio page or the LamaSky app's store listing. This notice may be updated when the website's practices change.

Scope: This page is a privacy notice, not an interactive forecast or an advertising page.
