Privacy Policy for LamaSky

Effective Date: July 25, 2026 • Engineered By: LamaStudio

Core Weather Privacy Guarantee: LamaSky is built with absolute local-storage weather data paradigms. We do not aggregate or harvest personal metrics or exact location coordinates on external cloud servers.

1. Zero Personal Data Harvesting

At LamaStudio, we believe your personal digital footprint belongs exclusively to you. LamaSky does not mandate account creation, user authentication portals, or credential submissions. Consequently, our ecosystem completely avoids collecting:

  • Legal names, home addresses, or cellular contact numbers.
  • Private email correspondence or messaging logs.
  • Sensitive local device storage, photos, or personal media files.

2. Local Weather & Coordinate Processing

To render live atmospheric updates, temperature scales, and dynamic home screen widgets seamlessly on your device, LamaSky processes geographical metrics strictly on-device:

  • Location Services: If location permissions are granted, coordinates are consumed locally to pull weather streams from verified open APIs. They are never mirrored or stored on LamaStudio mainframes.
  • Interface Preferences: Configuration choices, unit scaling (Celsius/Fahrenheit), and widget layouts persist securely within isolated local application storage.

3. Third-Party Telemetry & Analytics

To safeguard software performance, eliminate application crashes, and ensure system compatibility across complex mobile hardware, LamaSky may integrate standard telemetry frameworks (such as Google Play Services or AdMob networks). These systems may capture anonymous runtime metrics:

  • Aggregated diagnostic statistics and crash reports.
  • General hardware specification and operating system version IDs.
  • Broad regional identifiers optimized for network content delivery.

4. Minor & Family Security

LamaSky operates as an accessible family weather utility. We do not intentionally track, solicit, or gather information from minors under 13. If unauthorized telemetry submission is identified, contact our operations desk immediately for rapid data scrubbing.

5. Policy Revisions

This privacy declaration may evolve dynamically to mirror feature upgrades or weather system expansions. All future modifications will be published directly to this URL alongside a refreshed effective timestamp.
