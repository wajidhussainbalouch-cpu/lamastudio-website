Privacy Policy for LamaVPN Pro

Last updated & Effective Date: July 2026

🔒 1. Overview & Our Commitment

At LamaStudio, we believe privacy is an absolute human right. LamaVPN Pro is engineered from the ground up to secure your digital footprint without compromise. This Privacy Policy details the exact data principles, handling procedures, and technical protections applied when utilizing our mobile client application and global server infrastructure.

🛡️ 2. Uncompromising Zero-Logs Policy

LamaVPN Pro operates under a strict, verifiable zero-logs architecture. We explicitly do not track, collect, record, or share:

  • Your browsing history, websites visited, search queries, or application traffic contents.
  • DNS queries, domain resolution lookups, or intercepted network requests routed through our tunnels.
  • Original source IP addresses, individual connection timestamps, or session duration logs.
  • Bandwidth metrics linked to individual online activity or data packets transferred across our WireGuard node fleet.

Zero-Logs Guarantee: Because we do not log connection or traffic details, we have no user data to hand over to third parties, advertisers, or legal authorities even if formally requested.

🔑 3. Hardware-Backed Key Protection (Android Keystore)

Unlike conventional software applications that store sensitive cryptographic keys in vulnerable app directories or shared preferences files, LamaVPN Pro generates and secures your WireGuard private keys directly inside your device's dedicated hardware-backed Android Keystore subsystem.

Private keys remain entirely isolated within the device's secure execution hardware (TEE/StrongBox when available). Our architectural design ensures that even during elevated system debugging, root access, or process inspection, raw private cryptographic keys cannot be exported or extracted.

📊 4. Minimal Connection Metadata & Diagnostics

To maintain network stability, balance server loads, prevent platform abuse, and ensure high-speed routing across our global fleet, we process only minimal, anonymized telemetry:

  • Aggregate Session Status: General connection success/failure codes and total aggregate connection duration (without timestamps mapping to user identity).
  • Server Region Selection: The general geographic region or country node chosen for tunnel connection (e.g., Frankfurt, Tokyo, New York).
  • Account Entitlement Status: Anonymous subscription validation state managed securely through Google Play Billing.

This diagnostic metadata is stored in volatile memory, kept strictly isolated, and is never cross-referenced with your personal identity, email address, or browsing history.

💳 5. Third-Party Services, Monetization & Ads

LamaVPN Pro provides a reliable free tier supported by non-intrusive advertisements alongside an ad-free premium upgrade option:

  • Google AdMob: Free tier users may be served targeted advertisements managed via Google AdMob, which may collect non-personal advertising identifiers or device identifiers subject to Google’s privacy framework and user consent settings.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription transactions and Pro feature unlocks are processed directly and securely through Google Play Billing. LamaStudio does not store, process, or view your credit card numbers, banking details, or raw payment credentials.

⚙️ 6. User Control & Data Rights

As we store virtually no identifiable user traffic or browsing records, your account control is fully decentralized. You retain the right to uninstall the app at any time, clear app data locally, or manage/cancel subscriptions directly through your Google Play Store account settings.

🔄 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect structural network upgrades, feature enhancements, or legal compliance mandates. Any modifications will be posted directly within this document with an updated revision date.
