Executive Privacy Guarantee

LamaVPN Pro is built around an uncompromising zero-logs architecture and hardware-backed key protection. Your browsing data never touches our servers.

1. Overview & Our Commitment

At LamaStudio, we believe digital privacy is a fundamental human right. LamaVPN Pro is engineered from the ground up to protect your digital footprint, secure public Wi-Fi connections, and encrypt your internet traffic.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your data (or lack thereof) is handled when you use our mobile application, WireGuard tunnels, and web infrastructure.

2. Strict Zero-Logs Commitment

We operate under an absolute zero-logs architecture. This means we explicitly do not track, collect, record, or share:

  • Your browsing history, websites visited, or online traffic content.
  • DNS queries or lookups executed while connected to our secure tunnels.
  • Original source IP addresses or individual connection timestamps.
  • Bandwidth content or data packets transferred through our VPS fleet.

3. Hardware-Backed Key Protection

Unlike standard apps that store sensitive cryptographic data in vulnerable software directories, LamaVPN Pro generates and secures your WireGuard private keys directly inside the device's hardware-backed Android Keystore.

Private keys never leave the device’s secure execution environment, ensuring absolute protection against device compromise or process inspection.

4. Minimal Connection Metadata

To maintain load balancing, prevent abuse, and optimize real-time routing, we process only minimal, non-identifiable telemetry:

  • Aggregate session duration and connection success state.
  • General server location selection (country node).
  • Anonymous account subscription/billing status managed via Google Play Billing.

5. Third-Party Services & Monetization

LamaVPN Pro offers a functional free tier supported by non-intrusive advertisements alongside a premium upgrade option:

  • AdMob: Free tier users may view targeted advertisements managed via Google AdMob, which collects non-personal advertising identifiers governed by Google’s privacy framework.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription unlocks are processed securely through Google Play Billing infrastructure. LamaStudio does not store or process your credit card numbers.

6. Children's Privacy

LamaVPN Pro does not knowingly collect or solicit any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided such information, please contact us immediately.
