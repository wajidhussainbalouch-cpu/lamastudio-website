Privacy Policy for LamaVPN Pro

Last updated: July 2026

Zero-Logs Commitment

LamaVPN Pro operates under a strict zero-logs policy. We do not track, record, or store your browsing history, traffic destinations, data content, or DNS queries.

Data Collection & Connection Security

Minimal connection metadata (such as aggregate session duration and server location selection) is handled strictly for session optimization and load balancing, remaining entirely unlinked from your personal identity.

Hardware-Backed Key Protection

LamaVPN Pro utilizes the Android Keystore system to generate and secure your WireGuard cryptographic keys directly within device hardware, ensuring private keys never leave the secure environment.
