Privacy Policy for LamaVPN Pro

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview & Our Commitment

At LamaStudio, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. LamaVPN Pro is engineered from the ground up to protect your digital footprint. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data (or lack thereof) is handled when you use our mobile application and network infrastructure.

2. Strict Zero-Logs Policy

LamaVPN Pro operates under an uncompromising zero-logs architecture. We explicitly do not track, collect, record, or share:

  • Your browsing history, websites visited, or online traffic content.
  • DNS queries or lookups executed while connected to our tunnels.
  • Original source IP addresses or individual connection timestamps.
  • Bandwidth content or data packets transferred through our WireGuard VPS fleet.

3. Hardware-Backed Key Protection (Android Keystore)

Unlike standard applications that store sensitive cryptographic data in vulnerable software directories, LamaVPN Pro generates and secures your WireGuard private keys directly inside the device's hardware-backed Android Keystore system.

Private keys never leave the device’s secure execution environment. Our app architecture ensures that even in the event of an elevated device compromise or process inspection, raw private keys cannot be extracted or exported.

4. Minimal Connection Metadata & Diagnostics

To maintain load balancing, prevent abuse, and optimize real-time connection routing across our server fleet, we process only minimal, non-identifiable telemetry:

  • Aggregate session duration and connection success state.
  • General server location selection (e.g., country or region node).
  • Anonymous account subscription/billing status managed via Google Play Billing.

This technical metadata is kept strictly isolated and is never linked or cross-referenced with your personal identity or browsing activity.

5. Third-Party Services & Monetization

LamaVPN Pro offers a functional, free tier supported by non-intrusive advertisements alongside a premium upgrade option:

  • AdMob: Free tier users may view targeted advertisements managed via Google AdMob, which may collect non-personal advertising identifiers governed by Google’s privacy framework.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription and Pro unlocking mechanisms are processed securely through Google Play Billing infrastructure. LamaStudio does not store or process your credit card numbers or raw payment credentials.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements, structural network scaling, or legal compliance updates. Changes take effect immediately upon publication within this section.
