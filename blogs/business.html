Complete Guide to LamaStudio's 15 Essential Business & Financial Tools

Published: September 2026 By LamaStudio Editorial Team Read time: 9 mins

Sound financial modeling and operational metrics are vital for scaling sustainable ventures. Whether you are tracking monthly cash burn, calculating product profit margins, or analyzing SaaS lifetime values, having rapid access to reliable interactive calculators makes business planning efficient.

To support entrepreneurs and startup founders, LamaStudio has developed a comprehensive collection of 15 specialized business and financial utilities. This editorial guide covers how these key benchmarks apply directly to your growth strategy.

📈 Quick Access: You can explore and launch all of these utilities directly from our Business & Finance Hub.

Detailed Breakdown of the 15 Business Utilities

1. ROI & Profitability Calculator

Compute return on investment, net profit margins, and percentage gains to evaluate marketing campaigns or capital purchases.

2. Break-Even Point Analyzer

Determine exact unit sales volumes required to cover fixed and variable overhead costs.

3. Startup Burn Rate Calculator

Track gross and net monthly cash burn to monitor company spending velocity accurately.

4. CAGR Growth Estimator

Calculate compound annual growth rate over multi-year business cycles to measure historical performance.

5. Startup Cash Runway Tracker

Calculate months of operational survival remaining based on current cash reserves and burn rates.

6. Markup Calculator

Determine wholesale and retail selling prices based on item cost and target markup percentages.

7. Pricing & Margin Tool

Convert seamlessly between gross profit margins and product markups.

8. Loan & Amortization Planner

Compute monthly loan installments, total interest charges, and long-term repayment schedules.

9. Compound Growth Estimator

Project long-term asset growth and revenue compounding over successive operational periods.

10. SaaS Metrics Hub

Evaluate key customer lifetime value, churn rates, and monthly recurring revenue (MRR) health.

11. LTV Calculator

Measure Customer Lifetime Value to estimate long-term revenue generation per acquired user.

12. Salary & Payroll Tax Estimator

Estimate employee net take-home pay and calculate corresponding employer tax withholdings.

13. Invoice Total Calculator

Generate itemized invoice totals with applicable sales taxes, discounts, and shipping fees.

14. Invoice Discount & Terms Planner

Calculate effective annual interest rates for early payment terms (e.g., 2/10 net 30 structures).

15. Startup Valuation Estimator

Estimate preliminary pre-money valuations using revenue multiples and industry benchmarks.
