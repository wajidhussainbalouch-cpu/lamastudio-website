How to Check BISE DG Khan Examination Results

For verified SSC (9th and 10th) or HSSC (11th and 12th) results, use the website operated by the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Dera Ghazi Khan. This LAMA Studio page explains the process and links to the board; it does not retrieve, calculate or certify students' marks.

Open Official BISE DG Khan Website →

Steps to check your official result

  1. Open the official BISE DG Khan website using the link above.
  2. Find the result section for your examination, such as SSC or HSSC and the relevant annual examination.
  3. Enter the information requested by the board, usually your roll number, and submit the search on the board's website.
  4. Verify that the displayed examination, roll number and student information match your records.
  5. Save or print the result only from the official board system, and follow the board's instructions for detailed result cards or corrections.

Before you search

Keep your roll number slip or admission details available. A 9th-class result is different from a 10th-class result, and first annual and second annual examinations may have separate entries. Choose the correct examination on the board's site rather than relying on an unofficial result screenshot.

If a result is missing

First confirm that the board has announced that specific examination result. Double-check your roll number and selected session. On high-traffic result days, an official page can be slow. Retry the official site, review its current notices, or contact the board using the contact details it publishes. LAMA Studio cannot retrieve missing marks or change a board record.

What about other Punjab education boards?

Results for Multan, Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi and other boards are issued by their respective boards. Use the official website identified by the relevant board, rather than entering your details into an unrelated third-party page. The link above is specifically for BISE Dera Ghazi Khan.

Independence and privacy: LAMA Studio is not affiliated with BISE DG Khan. This guide does not ask for your roll number or name, and it does not display or verify any individual result. Only the official board should be relied upon for examination records and corrections.
