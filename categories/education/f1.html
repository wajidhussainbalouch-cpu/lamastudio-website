SECTION 3 — WRITING

Part 4 — Writing about a Topic. Choose ONE of these topics and write 100–150 words:

  • Describe a memorable trip you have taken. Explain where you went, what you did, and why you enjoyed it.
  • Write about a time when you solved a problem at work or school.
  • Describe your favorite restaurant and explain why you would recommend it.
  • Write about an important goal you have achieved and how you accomplished it.
