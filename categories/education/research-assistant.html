Academic Rigor Index

Complete these essential technical checks before sending your manuscript to your supervisor:

Run through institutional Plagiarism Scanner (e.g., Turnitin) & ensure similarity is below acceptable threshold (<15%).
Verify all in-text citations match the bibliography list precisely.
Check all figure captions, table headings, and equation numbers for sequential accuracy.
Ensure adherence to institutional formatting guidelines (Margins, Spacing, Font family).
Run grammar diagnostics and check for passive voice overuse.
Confirm Abstract covers objective, methodology, key findings, and conclusion.
