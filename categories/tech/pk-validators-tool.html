How do Mobile Wallet IBANs function?

Digital wallets operate under microfinance banks or electronic money institutions (EMIs). For example, JazzCash maps to Mobilink Microfinance Bank (MMBL), and EasyPaisa maps to Telenor Microfinance Bank. Entering your mobile number formats it cleanly into the required institutional routing structure.
