How to interpret the results

Ready: The local capability check worked on this device. Not ready: A capability or cached asset was unavailable. Failed: A tested operation did not work. Browser behavior may vary by Android version, memory, network and PWA installation mode.

For full release testing, compare results on Android Chrome and desktop Chrome/Firefox, repeat after an offline reload, and verify the real lesson flows. Anonymized downloads include check results only—not your name, email, code, saved notes, user identifiers or browser storage values.
