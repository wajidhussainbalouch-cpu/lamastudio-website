Getting started

How can I prepare for Headmaster or Headmistress jobs?

Open Question Bank Library, select your post, and open any numbered, published practice paper. Use My Study Plan to set daily study targets.

Are these official PPSC papers or confirmed syllabi?

No. The academy provides its own practice questions and suggested study topics. Always check the advertisement, eligibility, syllabus and case number on the official PPSC website.

How does the examination work?

A published paper contains 100 multiple-choice questions with a 90-minute timer. Select the paper you intend to attempt, start the examination, answer the questions and submit to view results. The paper's answer-key page is for reviewing a submitted practice attempt.

Where are my scores saved?

Practice attempts and progress are saved on this browser/device. When you are signed in and cloud saving succeeds, selected practice-history records may also be stored in your account. Do not assume results are synced until the app confirms a successful cloud save. Export your results before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Account & installation

How do I create or recover my account?

Choose Registration & Login. Register with an email address you can access, follow the email verification link, or choose Forgot Password to request a reset. Email delivery and sender branding must be verified before a paid launch.

How do I install the PWA on Android?

Open the academy in Chrome, tap the browser menu (⋮), and select Install app or Add to Home screen if available. The app can cache public practice resources for offline use, but account verification and server-side features require internet access.

Why do I still see the old app icon?

Open the website online, reload it and allow the new PWA cache to activate. Your Android launcher may retain an old shortcut icon. Before reinstalling, export any locally saved scores and attempts because uninstalling or clearing site data can delete progress.

Why is my job's examination missing?

The academy lists preparation tracks separately from completed examinations. A job appears in the selection menu when it is in the catalog; its practice papers appear only after its own question bank has been completed and published.

Membership & problems

Can I pay PKR 1,000 for 12 months now?

No. PKR 1,000 for 12 months is a planned membership offer, not an active payment product. No payment should be made until the academy explicitly announces an official activation method and provides a verified receipt and entitlement.

Where should I report an error?

For now, use the contact options published at the LAMA Studio website. Include the job, paper number, question ID, the issue and the browser version. Do not include passwords, recovery codes or sensitive candidate documents in a public report.
