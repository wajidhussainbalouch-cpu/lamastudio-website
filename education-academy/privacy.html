Privacy & Data Notice

LAMA PPSC Preparation Academy · Software · Education · Innovation

Pre-launch notice, 8 November 2026. This describes the currently implemented academy features. The owner must confirm a responsible contact, retention periods and applicable legal requirements before accepting paid members.

Information you provide

Account registration asks for your name, email, password and occupation, with optional gender, phone number, address and photo reference. The academy uses Supabase Authentication for account handling. Do not submit sensitive personal information in free-text feedback or question reports.

Practice progress and storage

Practice answers, timers, scores and study plans are saved locally in your browser. Your browser may delete them if you clear site data, change devices or remove the installed PWA. Signed-in candidates may separately save practice results in their own cloud account when the save succeeds.

Access to account data

The application database applies row-level access rules to candidate records and practice history. Authorized academy administrators may access account and registration details needed for support and administration. Public question resources are downloaded or cached by your device for practice.

Service providers and security

Supabase is used for candidate identity and database services; Vercel hosts the public website. A public browser configuration key is used for authorized API requests and is not an administrator secret. No internet service can promise perfect security, and you should use a unique, strong password and avoid sharing login codes.

Questions, corrections and data removal

Contact LAMA Studio through the official website to ask about your information. Before full paid launch, the owner must publish a verified contact method and the procedures for personal-data requests. You can remove local practice history using the academy's data controls or by clearing browser data, but clearing browser data does not automatically delete cloud account records.

Membership and payments

Paid memberships are not activated at this stage, and the academy does not provide a public payment-confirmation or premium entitlement process. Do not send money based on an unofficial message.
