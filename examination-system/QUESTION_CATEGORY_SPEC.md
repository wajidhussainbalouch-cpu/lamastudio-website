These are initial allocation budgets, NOT rigid quotas. Subject specialists must reallocate inapplicable types: e.g., Mathematics emphasizes calculations and word problems; Urdu/English emphasize reading and writing; Islamiat and Qaida require pronunciation and teacher review. Never fabricate an image-based question without its licensed/created image asset. Do not auto-grade oral pronunciation.

## Quality and release gates
1. Verify the actual supplied textbook edition, chapter, page and learning outcome before authoring.
2. Generate distinct, original questions; no mechanical paraphrase inflation or copied textbook exercises.
3. Each record needs stable question ID, class, subject, chapter/lesson, type, text, answer, explanation, difficulty, marks, scoring mode, version and review status.
4. Verify answers independently, including arithmetic, spelling, Urdu/Arabic script and distractor plausibility.
5. Detect exact and semantic near-duplicates within and across chapter batches.
6. Keep draft, reviewed, approved and online-ready counts separate. No draft is automatically published.
7. Protect answer keys in access-controlled storage before online publication; public GitHub JSON is not a secure answer-key store.
8. Verify teacher-approved scoring rubrics for descriptive and oral tasks.
9. Print and online formats share IDs and versions; protect school-specific examination records.
10. Super Admin independently licenses School Management, Examination/Question Bank and Smart Marking.
