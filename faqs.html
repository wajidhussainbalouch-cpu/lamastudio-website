Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LamaStudio, our products, tools, and community.

What tools and apps does LamaStudio offer?

LamaStudio offers a diverse ecosystem of tools, including LamaVPN Pro, LamaSky Weather, LamaIQMaster, LamaMultiCalendar, and LamaPhotoResizer, alongside interactive web research utilities designed for optimal performance.

How does the AskLama AI assistant work?

AskLama is your personal site guide powered by advanced AI. You can click our walking mascot or chat widget at any time to ask questions, find links, or navigate tools instantly.

Is LamaStudio free to use?

Yes! Most of our core web utilities, research generators, and community resources are entirely free to explore and utilize. Certain professional tiers or advanced features may have distinct specifications.

Where is LamaStudio based?

LamaStudio is proudly based in DG Khan, Pakistan, building resilient web applications and digital software tools for both domestic and global audiences.

How can I contact support or join the community?

You can reach out via email at contact@lamastudio.pk or join our active Facebook community and YouTube channel linked in the website footer.

How do I stay updated on new app releases?

You can bookmark our home dashboard, check our engineering blog section regularly, or follow our official social media channels on YouTube and Facebook for major rollout announcements.

Are my privacy and data secure when using your web utilities?

Yes. We prioritize user privacy. You can read through our detailed Privacy Policy page linked in the footer to understand how data is processed securely across our portal.

Can I contribute or collaborate with LamaStudio?

We love connecting with fellow developers, creators, and enthusiasts. Feel free to drop us an email with your portfolio, ideas, or proposals via contact@lamastudio.pk.
