LamaSky

a weather companion, rain detector and forecasting app.

Gregorian, Hijri, and Nanakshahi dates live side by side, kept in sync on your home screen — not just inside the app.

  • ✓Gregorian, Hijri, and Nanakshahi calendars in one view
  • ✓Home-screen Glance widgets with a three-layer sync strategy
  • ✓Full RTL layout and multilingual support
  • ✓Hilt-based architecture for fast, testable date logic

Under the hood

Date engine

Conversion logic for three calendar systems is isolated behind Hilt-injected interfaces, so widget, app, and notification code all read from the same source of truth.

Widgets

A three-layer sync strategy keeps home-screen widgets current even when the app hasn't been opened in days.

Language & layout

Full right-to-left layout support alongside multilingual strings, so the app reads naturally in Urdu as well as English.

Who it's for

Anyone tracking events across more than one calendar system — religious, cultural, or civic — without juggling three separate apps.
