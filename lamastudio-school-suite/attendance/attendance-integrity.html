Attendance Integrity Review

School-admin-only, read-only inspection of existing Google Sheets attendance records.

No records are changed here. If you see duplicates, do not press Save or Clear in the attendance register. Back up your school Google Spreadsheet before any manual reconciliation. Different statuses on the same date require human investigation.

Choose a date and analyze existing records. This page performs read requests only.

Review requirements

Compare the reported record IDs in the original Attendance and TeacherAttendance sheets. The report does not infer physical Google Sheet row numbers. Never assume identical-looking rows are safe to delete without a backup and checking creation times, edits and intended statuses.
