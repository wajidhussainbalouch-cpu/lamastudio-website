WhatsApp Business — connect your school

Use credentials belonging to this school, not LamaStudio. Create a WhatsApp Business Platform account in Meta Business Manager, register a sender, obtain a phone number ID and system-user access token with messaging permissions. Only approved templates can be sent outside the customer service window.

Checking connection…

Token is sent to the backend for verification and server-side storage. Do not share it in chats, spreadsheets or screenshots.

Send an approved template

Only students with recorded WhatsApp consent and valid numbers qualify. Meta acceptance does not confirm delivery. This initial sender supports templates without variables; personalized templates and delivery webhooks are future work.

⚙️ School communication settings

These preferences are stored in your school's data only. No WhatsApp messages are transmitted automatically until a secure official provider is connected.

Automatic WhatsApp — configuration requirements

Official WhatsApp Business Platform credentials, approved templates, parent opt-in, and a secure server-side message sender are required. This version intentionally does not collect tokens in the browser or claim that a bulk send succeeded.
