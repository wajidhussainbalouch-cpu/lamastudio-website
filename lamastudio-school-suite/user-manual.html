📘 LAMA School Suite — User Manual

A practical guide to the School Board, School Admin, Teacher, and Student/Parent portals. Search for a task, follow the steps, or ask LAMA for help.

What would you like to learn?

Ask LAMA gives general instructions; it does not access your school's private data or change records. Never enter passwords, student information or payment details in AI chat.

1. Getting started and signing in

Everyone
  1. Open your school's School Board. Use the School ID shown by the school to open the correct portal.
  2. Choose School Admin, Teacher, or Student/Parent. These are different logins with different permissions.
  3. Enter only your own authorized credentials. Teachers may need a class assignment before their classroom tools become available.
  4. If access fails, check the School ID, account type, connectivity, and whether the school administrator has activated your account.
Never share a password, reset link, administrator token, or another person's account in Ask LAMA or a support message.

2. School settings and curriculum

School Admin only
  1. Open School Settings from the top bar or the admin sidebar.
  2. Review the school profile, classes offered, and optional sections. Only enable the classes your school actually teaches.
  3. For each enabled class, tap subject buttons. Green means selected; unselected suggestions are not automatically added.
  4. Use ✎ Edit on a selected subject to change its display name, such as Art → Art & Craft. For Classes 9–12, group choices are suggestions, not automatic selections.
  5. Press Save & Broadcast; check the confirmation and then refresh the teacher dashboard to verify its shared subject list.
If saving fails or says it was not verified, inspect the current list before trying again. Features that are not yet deployed may not appear on your screen.

3. Admissions and student records

School Admin
  1. Open New Admissions or the Student Directory.
  2. Choose the class and enter the required enrollment and guardian details accurately.
  3. Before submitting, check that the student or enrollment number does not already exist.
  4. Wait for a saved confirmation, then refresh the class list to ensure the student is actually recorded.
  5. For larger lists, use the student import screen and review its preview, duplicate warnings and mapping before confirming.

4. Daily student attendance

Admin / Teacher
  1. Open Student Attendance as Admin, or the Attendance tab in your Teacher Portal.
  2. Choose the class and correct attendance date. Teachers should see only their assigned class.
  3. Mark each student Present, Absent, or Leave.
  4. Press Save. Check the saved status and class totals after records reload.
  5. Use the seven-day history under each student's name to notice repeated absences and contact a parent through your school's approved process.
A blank or unmarked date is not an absence. Do not treat button selections as saved until they are verified after saving.

5. Monthly attendance register and absentee follow-up

Admin / Teacher
  1. Open the Monthly Attendance Register.
  2. Choose the month and class; School Admin can review the whole school while a teacher sees the assigned class.
  3. Read P = Present, A = Absent, L = Leave, — = Not recorded. Sundays are shown separately; Saturday may be a working day.
  4. Use Print or Export CSV for a report and review the repeated-absence notices.
  5. Confirm your school's actual holiday calendar before contacting a family about a possible week's absence.
Open monthly register

6. Teachers and staff

School Admin
  1. Open Manage Teachers to review staff profiles and assigned classes.
  2. Check that each teacher has the correct class and access credentials; avoid accidental duplicate assignments.
  3. Use Teacher Attendance for staff Present, Absent or Leave and verify the save.
  4. Review staff follow-up separately from student attendance.

7. QR attendance

Admin / authorized Teacher
  1. Open the Student or Teacher Attendance page and locate its QR scanning panel if enabled for your school.
  2. Allow camera permission only when you intend to scan. Confirm the student/staff member and the date.
  3. Check the final saved attendance status instead of assuming a successful camera scan means the record was saved.
  4. If scanning fails, try browser permissions and an adequately lit code, then use the manual attendance option.

8. Academic calendar and class timetable

Admin edits / Teachers and students view their access
  1. Open Timetable & Academic Calendar. Admin first creates or chooses an academic session.
  2. Admin adds timetable periods with day, time, class, subject and optionally teacher/room.
  3. Add school holidays, examinations and events under Calendar Events; check overlapping dates and entries before saving.
  4. To correct a session, use Edit. Duplicate session names are blocked. A session linked to periods or events cannot simply be deleted.
  5. Choose All classes, Selected class or Class-wise separate pages before Print / Save as PDF. Check the school name and logo.
Open timetable & calendar

9. Homework and class subjects

Admin configures / Teacher publishes / Student views
  1. School Admin assigns each class's official subjects in School Settings and saves them.
  2. Teacher opens the Teacher Portal and checks Daily Subject Homework.
  3. Choose a subject, enter the homework title, instructions and due date, then publish it.
  4. After a saved record is verified for that posting date, the subject should show green. Red indicates no saved entry for that date; grey means verification is unavailable.
  5. Students and parents can check their class diary or homework from their authorized student portal.
A subject missing from the teacher list must be configured by the School Admin, rather than independently saved on the teacher's phone.

10. Examinations, assessments and results

Role-based
  1. School Admin uses the Assessments section to open the appropriate exam and report tools.
  2. Check the applicable class, selected subjects, assessment dates, question types and marking scheme before preparing papers.
  3. Teachers should enter or review marks only in their authorized workflows and verify saved results.
  4. Students/parents view their own published results in the Student Portal.
  5. Smart Marker/OMR availability and handwritten marking functionality vary by deployment; never treat an unverified scan as final marks.
AssessmentsStudent results

11. Fees, income and expenses

School Admin
  1. Open Fee Management to review class fee entries and saved collections.
  2. Verify student, amount, payment date and payment status before recording a receipt.
  3. Use the Finance Center to record and review other income/expenses.
  4. Compare month and year reports against actual saved transactions; dashboard counters are not proof of bank settlement.
Fee managementFinance center

12. Notices, parent communication and school updates

Role-based
  1. Admin uses the Communication Center or Notifications to review school messages.
  2. Choose the correct audience and check a notice before publishing it.
  3. Teachers use their assigned class notice/parent follow-up tools when permitted.
  4. Families can read their own school communications through the School Board and Student Portal.
An in-app notification is not the same as a delivered WhatsApp message. Do not assume parents were contacted unless delivery is confirmed.
Communication CenterNotifications

13. Teacher Portal — everyday routine

Teacher
  1. Check the assigned class, total students, boys and girls at the top of your dashboard.
  2. Review the date and saved Present/Absent/Leave/Unmarked totals.
  3. Mark and save attendance; follow up on repeated absences without assuming missing days were absences.
  4. Check which subjects have verified homework posted today and complete any remaining class work.
  5. Use Marks, Diary, Notices, and Employee ID tools as permitted by the school administrator.
My teacher dashboard

14. Student & Parent Portal

Student / Parent
  1. Sign in to the Student/Parent account issued by the school.
  2. Review your own student ID, class, diary, results and notifications where published.
  3. Check teacher and class information; ask the school when a published entry looks incorrect.
  4. Use the School Board for published notices, events and updates.

15. Identity cards and certificates

Role-based

Admin tools can prepare appropriate school identity and certificate materials. Teachers can view their own employee identity card and students can access their own student card or published certificate information where available.

Before printing, confirm spelling, school name, photo and the intended recipient. Do not send a student's identity card to an unrelated user.

Identity card tools

16. When something is not working

Everyone
  1. Check your internet connection, school ID, role and selected class/date.
  2. Read the message shown after Save. Refresh only after a saved operation is confirmed; if a save is uncertain, inspect existing records before trying again.
  3. If a subject is missing, ask your School Admin to update the curriculum. If a report is blank, verify its class, month and role filters.
  4. For repeated entries, use the provided Edit/Delete controls only after reviewing the linked records. Never remove data simply to clear an error.
  5. Use Ask LAMA for guidance. If you still cannot complete a task, the School Admin can open Help & Support and submit a ticket to the Super Admin.
