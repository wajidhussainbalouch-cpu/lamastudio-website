Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026. This policy explains what LamaStudio collects, why, and how it's handled — across lamastudio.pk and all LamaStudio Android apps.

1. Who we are

LamaStudio is an independent Android app studio based in Pakistan, operating lamastudio.pk and the apps listed on it. This policy covers this website and every LamaStudio app unless that app's own in-app privacy notice says otherwise.

2. Information we collect

What's collected depends on the app or page, but generally falls into these categories:

  • Account & license data — email address and license/trial status for apps with a Pro tier.
  • Payment claim data — transaction ID and payment method (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or Raast) when you submit a manual payment claim. We do not receive your mobile wallet PIN or full account credentials.
  • Usage & diagnostics — crash reports, app version, and device model, used only to fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Content you provide directly — for tools like lamaPhotoResizer, the images or files you choose to process. Where processing happens on-device, that content never leaves your device.
  • Website analytics — standard, non-identifying traffic data (pages visited, approximate region, device type) for lamastudio.pk.

3. How we use information

  • To verify licenses and activate Pro features after a successful payment claim.
  • To provide the core function of an app (e.g. processing a photo, generating a result card, routing VPN traffic).
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • To respond to support requests sent to hello@lamastudio.pk.
  • To display advertising in free-tier apps and on lamastudio.pk, where applicable.

4. Third-party services

Depending on the app, LamaStudio uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising in free-tier apps.
  • Google Play Billing / RevenueCat — in-app purchase processing.
  • Firebase — hosting for premium content and, where used, crash reporting.
  • EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and Raast — payment rails for manual payment claims; each processor handles your transaction under its own policy.

5. Data security

Where apps handle sensitive material — such as VPN keys in LamaVPN Pro — it's generated on-device and stored using Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. Server-side data (license records, payment claims) is access-controlled and not sold or shared with data brokers.

6. Children's privacy

LamaStudio apps are not directed at children under 13. Education-focused tools intended for use by schools (such as Wajid Result Maker) are designed for use by teachers and administrators, not for direct data collection from students.

7. Your choices

  • You can request deletion of your account or license data by emailing hello@lamastudio.pk.
  • Most LamaStudio apps can be used, at least in a free tier, without creating an account.
  • Ad personalization can typically be limited through your device's ad settings (Android Settings → Privacy → Ads).

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as apps or services change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "last updated" date.

Questions about this policy or a specific app's data handling? Email hello@lamastudio.pk.
