What we build

Services

LamaStudio takes on a small number of outside projects alongside its own apps — usually for teams that need an Android build done properly the first time, or a billing setup that actually works for Pakistani users.

Android app development

Native builds in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, from a single screen to a full MVVM app with Room, Hilt, and Play Billing. We handle architecture and UI together, not one after the other.

Local payment integration

EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and Raast wired into your subscription flow alongside Google Play Billing, so a freemium model actually converts in the local market.

Backend & API work

REST APIs, auth flows, and the server-side plumbing behind a mobile app — sized to what the app needs, not a generic template stack.

Web presence for apps

Landing pages and app-store-style storefronts, built with the same design system across every product page, from initial concept through to SEO-ready launch.

How an engagement works

  • Short discovery call to scope the app or feature and confirm what "done" looks like.
  • A working build shared early, not just at the end — usually within the first week.
  • Fixed-price or milestone billing, agreed before any code is written.
  • Handover includes source, signing keys, and a plain-language note on what to maintain.
