Four things we're good at

How engagements work

Most projects start as a short scoping conversation to pin down what the software actually needs to do, who it's for, and what "done" looks like. From there:

  • Scope & architecture — a written plan covering data model, tech stack, and a realistic timeline before any code is written.
  • Build in visible stages — working versions delivered incrementally, not a single release at the end.
  • Launch support — Play Store submission, deployment, and a defined window of post-launch fixes included in every engagement.
  • Ongoing maintenance — optional retainer for updates, new features, and monitoring after launch.
